Why it works

One stronger story

The calculator demonstrates product thinking, interaction design, domain care, and implementation—not a separate hobby category.

Naming

“Use now” beats “Tool”

The filter describes what the visitor can do, while “case study” describes the alternate project-card behavior.

The cost

Mixed project scale

A calculator and a full-stack tracker are different weights. The oversized live card makes that difference explicit instead of hiding it.

Signature

Live project specimen
