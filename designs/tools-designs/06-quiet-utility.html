Why it works

Memorable restraint

The cost

Weak first discovery

Many visitors will never open it. This cannot be the only route from the related article or homepage.

Accessibility

Button, not mystery meat

Use the visible “Useful things” label, an expanded state, escape-to-close, focus return, and no keyboard-only assumptions.

Signature

The utility key

A tiny persistent control feels like a maker’s workbench drawer—appropriate for a developer site, but not styled like a generic chat widget.
